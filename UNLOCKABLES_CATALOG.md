# 🎮 UNLOCKABLES CATALOG
**Complete Guide to Angry Animals Unlockable Modifiers**

This catalog documents all unlockable gameplay modifiers, their effects, unlock conditions, and usage guidelines for non-coders.

---

## 🏆 OVERVIEW

Angry Animals features **6 unlockable gameplay modifiers** that change how the game feels and plays. These modifiers are unlocked through organic gameplay achievements and can be toggled on/off in the Settings menu.

**Key Points:**
- All modifiers are **completely optional** - game works perfectly without them
- They **stack and combine** for different experiences
- Can be **toggled mid-game** for immediate effect
- **Hardcore Mode** disables all modifiers for pure gameplay

---

## 🎭 MODIFIER DETAILS

### 1. **EXTREME PHYSICS** 🌪️

**What it does:**
- Doubles ragdoll chaos and intensity
- Limbs fly farther and spin more wildly
- Explosions are more dramatic and impactful
- Projectiles bounce higher and more unpredictably
- Overall "physics feel" becomes much more chaotic and fun

**How to unlock:**
- Win **20 levels** without using the slingshot a second time
- "No second shot" means one projectile per level maximum
- Perfect for players who want to play efficiently

**Visual effect:**
- Ragdoll limbs spin like helicopter blades
- Explosions create bigger particle effects
- Characters fly through air more dramatically
- Impact sounds are more intense

**Multiplier stacking:**
- Combines with **Double Explosions** → 4x explosion intensity (capped at 3x max)
- Combines with **Big Heads** → Larger heads fly even more dramatically
- Combines with **No Gravity** → Floating chaos physics

**Toggleable:** ✅ YES - Settings → Modifiers tab

### 4. **SLOW MOTION** ⏰

- Rewards persistent play and exploration

- Time tokens display in HUD during gameplay
- Perfect for lining up difficult shots

**Visual effect:**

- Projectile trails become visible
- Ragdoll physics move in slow, dramatic arcs
- Screen gets subtle blue tint during slow motion

**Multiplier stacking:**
- Works well with any other modifier

- Helpful for mastering **Big Heads** comedy timing

**Toggleable:** ✅ YES - Settings → Modifiers tab

## 🔧 HOW TO USE MODIFIERS

### **Accessing Modifiers:**
1. From **Main Menu**, click **Settings**
2. Go to **Modifiers** tab
3. See all 6 modifiers with lock/unlock status

### **Unlocking Process:**
- **Progress tracking** happens automatically
- **Unlock notifications** appear when you earn new modifiers
- **Progress bars** show how close you are to each unlock
- **No need to do anything special** - just play naturally

### **Toggling Modifiers:**
- **Green toggle = ON** (modifier active)
- **Red toggle = OFF** (modifier inactive)
- **Gray toggle = LOCKED** (not unlocked yet)
- **Changes apply immediately** - no restart required

### **Hardcore Mode:**
- **One toggle disables all modifiers** for pure gameplay
- Perfect for **speedrunning** or **challenge runs**
- **No effect on unlock progress** - can still unlock while hardcore mode is on

## ❓ TROUBLESHOOTING

### **"I unlocked a modifier but it doesn't seem to work!"**
- Check that the toggle is **green (ON)** in Settings
- Some modifiers only affect new gameplay, not existing ragdolls
- Try **restarting the level** for full effect

### **"I can't see my unlock progress!"**
- Progress tracking is automatic - just keep playing!
- Check the **Modifiers tab** in Settings for progress bars
- **No manual tracking needed**

### **"Are modifiers required to finish the game?"**
- **NO!** All modifiers are completely optional
- Game is fully playable and completable without any modifiers
- Modifiers just add variety and fun for replay value

### **"Can I unlock modifiers while playing hardcore mode?"**
- **YES!** Hardcore mode only disables modifier effects
- You can still unlock new modifiers while hardcore mode is on
- Toggle modifiers on after unlocking to use them

### **"Do modifiers affect my completion time for leaderboards?"**
- **Yes** - modifiers create different game experiences
- **Hardcore Mode** is recommended for speedrunning leaderboards
- Consider running separate categories: "With Modifiers" vs "Hardcore"
