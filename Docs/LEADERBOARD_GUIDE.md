# Leaderboard Features Guide

Angry Animals tracks local high scores and milestones to encourage replayability.

## High Score Tracking
- **Top 10**: The game stores the top 10 scores for every level in `user://leaderboard.json`.
- **Details**: Entries include Player Name, Score, and Date.

## Milestone Celebrations
The game celebrates the following milestones:
- **Novice**: 100,000 total points.
- **Star Collector**: 10 levels 3-starred.
- **Perfectionist**: All 100 levels 3-starred.
- **Millionaire**: 1,000,000 total points.

## Social Sharing
Players can share their best scores and star ratings via the system share sheet (Mobile only).
