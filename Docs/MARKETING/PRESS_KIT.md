### Potential Questions & Responses

**Q: What makes Yeet Face different from Angry Birds?**
A: "While Angry Birds has you throwing birds, Yeet Face makes YOU the character. You see your own face, the AI comments on your appearance, and perfect scores trigger hilarious meme mini-games that are perfect for sharing on social media."

**Q: How does the face customization work?**
A: "Players upload their photo, and our system integrates it into the character model. The AI then generates unique dialogue based on the player's appearance, creating a truly personalized experience that differs for every person."

**Q: What's the viral potential of the meme mini-games?**
A: "Perfect score achievements trigger absurd celebration animations that are naturally shareable. We've designed these moments specifically for TikTok, Twitter, and other platforms where unexpected humor goes viral."

**Q: How can people create NPCs without coding?**
A: "Our drag-and-drop system lets anyone add characters to levels. You simply drag a prefab into the scene, write dialogue in the Inspector, apply cosmetics, and you've created a custom character. No programming required."

**Q: What's your target audience?**
A: "Physics game enthusiasts who want something fresh, content creators looking for viral moments, and anyone who's ever wanted to see themselves as a game character. It's also perfect for educators teaching game development."

**Q: How do you plan to monetize this?**
A: "We're focusing on building a strong community first. Future monetization could include cosmetic packs, additional meme variants, or premium NPC creation tools - all while keeping the core experience free and accessible."
