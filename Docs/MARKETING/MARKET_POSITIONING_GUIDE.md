# Market Positioning Guide

## Unique Selling Point (USP)
The **Emoting Head Character** is the primary differentiator. Unlike other physics puzzlers with static characters, Angry Animals uses the player's own face (or a custom one) that reacts authentically to the physics of the game.

## Marketing Highlights
- **Be the Star**: Use your own face as the projectile.
- **Dynamic Emotions**: Watch your face react to launches, crashes, and victories.
- **Infinite Levels**: Daily challenges and procedural generation ensure you never run out of content.
- **Satisfying Physics**: High-quality destruction and platforming traversal.

## ASO Keywords
Physics Puzzler, Face Game, Custom Character, Angry Birds alternative, Daily Challenge, Destruction, Platformer, Emoting Character.
