#### 1. **POV Videos** (HIGH ENGAGEMENT)
```

## 🐦 TWITTER/X STRATEGY

### Tweet Templates

#### 1. **Absurdist Humor**
```
Template: "Breaking: Local [character] discovers [game mechanic]"

Examples:
- "Breaking: Local Mom has been saying 'WATCH IT!' this entire time"
- "Scientists confirm: Dad's military helmet actually makes him more authoritative"
- "URGENT: Schoolmate trapped in cage has some THOUGHTS"
- "Alert: Perfect scores now trigger UNHINGED memes"

Engagement: Ask for responses, quotes, reactions
Timing: Peak hours (12-2 PM, 7-9 PM EST)
```

#### 2. **Developer Commentary**
```
Template: "Day [X] of [activity]: [specific update]"

Examples:
- "Day 15 of launch: Added 50 new dialogue lines via Inspector (no code)"
- "Day 3 of community feedback: Player requested 'alien_antenna' cosmetic - done!"
- "Day 1 of streamer outreach: 5 streamers already playing voluntarily"
- "Day 7 of viral content: TikTok videos hitting 100k+ views"

Engagement: Ask for suggestions, share behind-the-scenes
Timing: Throughout the day
```

#### 3. **Game Feature Highlights**
```
Template: "Here's why [feature] is different from [competitor]"

Examples:
- "Here's why Yeet Face is different from Angry Birds: You see YOUR face"
- "Here's why our meme mini-games work: Perfect scores trigger absurd celebrations"
- "Here's why non-coders can customize: Inspector-based NPC system"

Engagement: Quote tweets, comparison discussions
Timing: 10 AM, 3 PM, 8 PM EST
```

### Twitter Engagement Tactics
```
Reply Strategies:
- Quote tweet gameplay clips with commentary
- Reply to gaming influencers' posts
- Engage with #IndieGame community

Thread Strategy:
1. Hook tweet with intriguing statement

4. End with call-to-action

## 🚨 CRISIS MANAGEMENT

### Negative Feedback Response
```
1. Acknowledge quickly (within 1 hour)
2. Understand the concern
3. Provide solution or timeline
4. Follow up with resolution

Response Templates:
- "Thanks for the feedback! We're looking into this."
- "We appreciate the concern. Here's what we're doing about it."
- "You're absolutely right. We're implementing changes now."
```

### Viral Misunderstanding
```
1. Clarify intent immediately
2. Provide context if needed
3. Engage with community discussion
4. Turn into educational moment

Prevention:
- Clear communication
- Community guidelines
- Content warnings when needed
- Proactive community management
```
