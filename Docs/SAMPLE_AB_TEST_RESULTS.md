# Sample A/B Test Results

## Battle Pass Pricing Test Results
**Test Period:** January 1-14, 2024 (14 days)  
**Total Users:** 7,485  
**Target Metric:** Conversion Rate  

### Test Variants
| Variant | Price | Users Assigned | Conversions | Conversion Rate | Uplift vs Control |
|---------|-------|--------------|------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Control | $3.99 | 2,495 | 205 | 8.2% | - |
| Variant A | $4.99 | 2,491 | 284 | 11.4% | +39.0% |
| Variant B | $5.99 | 2,499 | 195 | 7.8% | -4.9% |

### Statistical Analysis
- **Winner:** Variant A ($4.99 price)
- **Statistical Confidence:** 97.2%
- **P-value:** 0.028 (statistically significant)
- **Revenue Impact:** +$2,847/month additional revenue

### Business Decision
✅ **Implement $4.99 price globally**  
🎯 **Expected annual impact:** +$34,164 additional revenue

## Notification Time Test Results
**Test Period:** January 1-28, 2024 (28 days)  
**Total Users:** 8,932  
**Target Metric:** D1 Retention  

### Test Variants
| Variant | Send Time | Message Type | Users | D1 Retention | D7 Retention |
|---------|----------|--------------|-------|--------------|-------------|
| Control | 9:00 AM | Standard | 2,977 | 72% | 38% |
| Variant A | 7:00 AM | Personalized | 2,978 | 68% | 35% |
| Variant B | 11:00 AM | Emojis | 2,977 | 74% | 41% |

### Key Insights
- **Early morning (7 AM):** Lower engagement, users sleeping
- **Late morning (11 AM):** Better engagement, users active
- **Personalization:** Underperformed expectations
- **Emoji messages:** Most engaging for target demographic

### Business Decision
✅ **Implement 11 AM send time with emoji messages**  
📈 **Expected D7 retention improvement:** +8%
