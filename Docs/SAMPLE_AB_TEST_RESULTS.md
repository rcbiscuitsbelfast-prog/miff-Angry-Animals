# Sample A/B Test Results - Real-World Examples

## Battle Pass Pricing A/B Test Results

### Test Configuration
- **Test Name**: Battle Pass Pricing Optimization
- **Duration**: 21 days (January 15 - February 5, 2024)
- **Sample Size**: 15,000 players
- **Primary Metric**: Conversion Rate (Battle Pass Purchase %)
- **Secondary Metrics**: ARPPU, D7 Retention

### Test Variants
| Variant | Price | Users | Purchases | Conversion Rate | ARPPU | Uplift vs Control |
|---------|-------|-------|-----------|----------------|-------|-------------------|
| Control | $4.99 | 5,000 | 115 | 2.3% | $12.50 | - |
| Variant A | $3.99 | 5,000 | 155 | 3.1% | $10.80 | +35% |
| Variant B | $5.99 | 5,000 | 90 | 1.8% | $16.20 | -22% |

### Statistical Significance
- **Confidence Level**: 95%
- **P-value**: 0.0023 (highly significant)
- **Winner**: Variant A ($3.99)
- **Recommendation**: Switch to $3.99 pricing immediately

### Business Impact
- **Revenue Increase**: +$1,200/week (+35% conversion rate)
- **Annual Impact**: +$62,400 additional revenue
- **Risk Assessment**: Low - minimal retention impact (-2% D7 retention)

### Business Impact

- **Retention Risk**: -6% D7 retention (acceptable)
- **Net Impact**: +$121,680 additional annual revenue
