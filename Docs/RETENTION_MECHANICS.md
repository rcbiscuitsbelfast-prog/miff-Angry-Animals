# Player Retention Mechanics

Angry Animals employs several loops to keep players coming back.

## 1. The Daily Loop
The **Daily Challenge** provides a reason to open the app every day. The deterministic nature allows friends to compete on the exact same level.

## 2. The Progression Loop
- **Star Hunting**: Replaying levels to get 3 stars.
- **Unlocks**: Advancing through 100 hand-crafted levels.
- **Cosmetics**: Earning or buying new skins to customize the experience.

## 3. The Emotional Hook
The **Emoting Head** creates a personal connection. Players are more invested in the success of a character that has their own face and shows visible distress when they fail.

## 4. Push Notifications
- **Midnight Reset**: "Today's Daily Challenge is ready!"
- **Milestone Reached**: "You're 5 levels away from being a Star Collector!"
- **New Content**: Notifications about new cosmetic packs.
