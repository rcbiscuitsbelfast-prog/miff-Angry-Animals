# Social Leaderboard System - User Guide

## Overview

Global and friend leaderboards create competitive rankings that drive engagement and viral growth through replay sharing and friend challenges.

---

## Viewing Leaderboards

### Accessing Leaderboards

**From Main Menu:**
1. Tap "Leaderboards" button
2. Choose tab: Global / Friends / By Level
3. View rankings with live updates

### Leaderboard Types

**1. By Level (Top 100 per level):**
- Highest scores on specific levels
- Filter by level selection
- Your rank highlighted
- Friend ranks marked with icons

**2. Total Score (Cumulative):**
- Sum of all level high scores
- Global top 100 players
- Your total score + rank
- Climb by improving any level

**3. Perfect Levels (5-Star Count):**
- Players with most 5-star completions
- Demonstrates skill mastery
- Each 5-star counts once
- Encourages perfection

**4. Replay Views (Viral Leaders):**
- Most-watched replays
- Encourages sharing quality content
- Social media integration
- Content creator rankings

---

## Finding Yourself on Leaderboards

### Quick Rank Check

**View Your Rank:**
1. Open any leaderboard
2. Your entry auto-highlights in gold
3. Swipe to scroll to your position
4. "Jump to Me" button (top-right)

**Rank Display:**
- 🥇 **#1**: Gold medal icon
- 🥈 **#2**: Silver medal
- 🥉 **#3**: Bronze medal
- **#4-10**: Purple highlight
- **#11-100**: Standard entry
- **Below 100**: "Not ranked yet" with nearest rank

---

## Friend Rankings

### Friend Filter

**Viewing Friends Only:**
1. Toggle "Friends" filter (top-right)
2. Shows only friends from your list
3. Compare directly with people you know
4. Challenge friends from leaderboard

**Friend Indicators:**
- 👥 Icon next to friend names
- Online status (green dot)
- Last seen timestamp
- Direct challenge button

---

## Watching Top Player Replays

### Replay Integration

**View Replay Button:**
- Available for all top 100 players
- Watch their perfect runs
- Learn strategies
- Copy techniques

**Challenge from Leaderboard:**
1. Tap any player entry
2. Tap "Challenge" button
3. Sends friend challenge (if friend)
4. Or "Try to Beat" (if stranger)

---

## Climbing the Leaderboard

### Strategy Guide

**Improving Your Rank:**
1. **Focus on Total Score**: Improve all levels, not just one
2. **Get Perfect Levels**: 5-star completions boost multiple leaderboards
3. **Share Replays**: Viral replays increase Replay Views rank
4. **Accept Challenges**: Practice makes perfect

**Optimization:**
- Identify your weakest levels
- Watch top player replays for tips
- Use optimal cosmetics (some give slight advantages)
- Play during events for bonus points

## Leaderboard Updates

### Real-Time Sync

**Update Frequency:**
- Syncs every 5 minutes automatically
- Manual refresh: pull-down gesture
- Firebase real-time updates (if online)
- Offline mode: shows cached data

**Cache System:**
- Top 100 cached locally
- Works offline
- Refreshes when online
- Conflict resolution: server is truth
