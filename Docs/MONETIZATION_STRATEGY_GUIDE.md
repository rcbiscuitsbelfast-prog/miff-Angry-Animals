## Revenue Overview

### Revenue Sources

```
Total Revenue = (Cosmetics Sales) + (Battle Pass) + (Ads) + (Other IAP)

Cosmetics Sales = DAU × Conversion Rate × ARPPU
Battle Pass = DAU × BP Conversion Rate × BP Price
```

|--------|--------------|-------|
| Cosmetics | 40-50% | Primary driver |
| Battle Pass | 20-30% | Recurring revenue |
| Ads | 20-30% | Non-paying users |
| Other IAP | 5-10% | Remove ads, etc. |

## Pricing Strategy

### Cosmetic Price Tiers

| Rarity | Coin Price | USD Price | Rationale |
|--------|------------|-----------|-----------|
| Common | 0-100 | Free | Entry point, build habit |
| Uncommon | 500-1000 | - | Low barrier, "first purchase" |
| Rare | 1500-2500 | - | Medium commitment |
| Epic | 3000-5000 | $1.99-$2.99 | Premium items |
| Legendary | - | $3.99-$4.99 | Whales, collectors |

### Price Psychology Principles

#### 1. The $4.99 Sweet Spot

- Feels like a "treat" not an investment
- Less psychological friction than $5.00
- Under $10 avoids "big purchase" evaluation

#### 2. Anchoring

**Show expensive items first:**
```
$9.99 Legendary Crown ← This seems expensive
$4.99 Epic Axe ← This seems reasonable
$2.99 Rare Shield ← Great value!
```

#### 3. The Decoy Effect

**Create obvious choices:**
```
Basic Pack: $2.99 (10 cosmetics)
Deluxe Pack: $4.99 (30 cosmetics) ← BEST VALUE
Premium Pack: $9.99 (50 cosmetics)
```

#### 4. Freemium Psychology

**Free items serve a purpose:**
- "I got something for free = this game is generous"
- "I want to earn more free stuff"
- Increases time in app → more ad impressions

### Currency Bundle Pricing

| Coins | USD | Price per 1000 |
|-------|-----|----------------|
| 500 | $0.99 | $1.98 |
| 1100 | $1.99 | $1.81 |
| 2800 | $4.99 | $1.78 |
| 6000 | $9.99 | $1.67 |

**Bundle discount increases with size!**

## Seasonal Strategy

### Why Seasonal Works

1. **Urgency** - "Buy now or miss out!"
2. **FOMO** - Fear of Missing Out drives conversions
3. **Novelty** - New items = new reasons to engage
4. **Content rhythm** - Players know when to expect new stuff

### Seasonal Calendar

| Season | Theme | Months | Focus |
|--------|-------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Ice/Winter | Jan-Feb | Post-holiday, fresh start |
| 2 | Fire/Spring | Apr-May | Renewal energy |
| 3 | Nature/Summer | Jul-Aug | Vacation, outdoor |
| 4 | Dark/Autumn | Oct-Oct | Spooky, Halloween |

### Seasonal Cosmetics Count

| Season | New Cosmetics | % Exclusive |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| Launch | 20+ | 100% |
| Season 1 | 10 | 40% new |
| Season 2 | 10 | 40% new |
| Season 3 | 10 | 40% new |
| Season 4 | 10 | 40% new |

**Keep 60% of cosmetics evergreen!**

### Limited-Time Tactics

**Countdown timers:**
```
"Limited Time Offer"
"Returns in: 23:59:59"
```

**Exclusive messaging:**
```
"Only available this season!"
"Collect before they're gone forever!"
```

### Season Launch Timing

**Optimal pattern:**
1. **Week 1**: Tease upcoming cosmetics
2. **Week 2**: Season launch, maximum push
3. **Week 3**: Mid-season sale, "last chance"
4. **Week 4**: Final push, urgency messaging

## Battle Pass Optimization

### Battle Pass Pricing

| Price | Conversion | Revenue/1000 DAU |
|-------|------------|------------------|
| $2.99 | 8% | $239/mo |
| $4.99 | 5% | $250/mo |
| $7.99 | 3% | $240/mo |

**$4.99 is the sweet spot!**

### Battle Pass Value Proposition

**Perceived Value:**
- 500+ premium coins = ~$5.00
- 10 exclusive cosmetics = ~$30.00
- **Total perceived: $35+**
- **Actual price: $4.99**
- **Player savings: $25+**

### Battle Pass Rewards Balance

| Tier | Free Reward | Premium Reward |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| 1-5 | 100-500 coins | 200-1000 coins |
| 6-10 | XP boost | Premium cosmetic! |
| 11-15 | Coins | Coins + Premium |
| 16-20 | 500 coins | Premium cosmetic! |
| 21-25 | XP boost | Premium currency |
| 26-30 | Big coins | Ultimate cosmetic! |

### Free vs Premium Rewards

| Aspect | Free Track | Premium Track |
|--------|------------|---------------|
| Tiers | 20 | 30 |
| Cosmetics | 0 | 10 |
| Premium Currency | 0 | 500+ |
| Purpose | Engagement | Conversion |

### XP Progression

**Recommended XP per tier:**

| Tier | XP Required | Play Time |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| 1-5 | 100 | ~1 level/day |
| 6-15 | 100-120 | ~2 levels/day |
| 16-25 | 120-140 | ~2-3 levels/day |
| 26-30 | 140-150 | ~3 levels/day |

**Don't make it too grindy! Players should finish with effort, not frustration.**

## Conversion Rate Optimization

### Conversion Funnel

```
1. Shop Open (100%)
    ↓
2. Browse Items (80%)
    ↓
3. View Details (40%)
    ↓
4. Consider Purchase (20%)
    ↓
5. Purchase (5%)
```

### Optimization Tactics

#### At Shop Open (100% → 80%)

**What works:**
- Attractive featured items
- "New" badges
- Clear value proposition

#### During Browse (80% → 40%)

**What works:**
- Beautiful thumbnails
- Rarity colors visible
- Quick "owned" check

#### View Details (40% → 20%)

**What works:**
- Large preview
- Clear price
- Social proof ("50,000 players own this!")

#### Consider (20% → 5%)

**What works:**
- Price anchor (show original price)
- "Best value" badges
- Limited time urgency

### Purchase Confirmation

**Reduce friction:**
- Pre-selected coin bundle if needed
- One-tap purchase for small amounts
- Clear refund policy (if applicable)

### Exit Intent

**Before player leaves:**
```
"Wait! Here's 20% off your first purchase!"
[Claim 20% Off]
[No thanks, leave]
```

## A/B Testing Framework

### What to Test

| Variable | Options | Impact |
|----------|---------|--------|
| Battle Pass Price | $2.99 vs $4.99 vs $7.99 | High |
| Cosmetic Price | Low vs High | Medium |
| Free Rewards | More vs Better | Medium |
| Visual Prominence | Big vs Subtle | Low-Medium |

### Running A/B Tests

**Sample size calculator:**
```
For 5% conversion difference at 95% confidence:
- Need ~3,000 users per variant
- Run for 2-4 weeks
```

**Implementation:**

```csharp
// Assign test group
var testGroup = GetABTestGroup("bp_price_test");

switch (testGroup)
{
    case "price_299":
        BattlePassPrice = 2.99f;
        break;
    case "price_499":
        BattlePassPrice = 4.99f;
        break;
    case "price_799":
        BattlePassPrice = 7.99f;
        break;
}

// Track conversion
AnalyticsManager.LogEvent("ab_test", new {
    test_name = "bp_price_test",
    group = testGroup,
    converted = purchased
});
```

### Test Prioritization Matrix

| Test | Effort | Expected Impact | Priority |
|------|--------|-----------------|----------|
| BP Price | Low | High | #1 |
| Free BP tiers | Medium | High | #2 |
| Cosmetic bundles | Low | Medium | #3 |
| Shop layout | High | Low | Low |
| Color scheme | Low | Low | Low |

## Common Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1: Pricing Too High

**Problem:** High prices = low conversion
**Solution:** Start low, raise after testing

### Pitfall 2: Too Expensive for F2P

**Problem:** F2P players can't earn enough
**Solution:** Balance earn rate with shop prices

### Pitfall 3: No Urgency

**Problem:** "I'll buy later" = never buys
**Solution:** Limited time offers, seasons

### Pitfall 4: Paywalling Everything

**Problem:** Players feel exploited
**Solution:** 50%+ content should be free

### Pitfall 5: Ignoring Analytics

**Problem:** Can't optimize what you don't measure
**Solution:** Track everything, review weekly

### Pitfall 6: Same Prices Forever

**Problem:** Inflation and opportunity missed
**Solution:** Quarterly price reviews

## Quick Reference: Revenue Formulas

```
Conversion Rate = Paying Users / DAU
ARPPU = Total Revenue / Paying Users
ARPDAU = Total Revenue / DAU
LTV = Average Revenue per User over Lifetime
LTV (Simplified) = ARPPU × Number of Purchases
```
