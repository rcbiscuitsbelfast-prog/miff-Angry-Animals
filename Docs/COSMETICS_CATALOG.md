# Cosmetics Catalog

Angry Animals features a wide range of cosmetics available via In-App Purchases (IAP).

## Slingshot Skins ($0.99 each)
- **Gold**: Shiny gold finish with metallic reflections.
- **Crystal**: Translucent blue with glowing edges.
- **Fire**: Animated flames licking the base.
- **Ice**: Frozen texture with frost particles.

## Projectile Skins ($0.99 each)
- **Angry**: Furrowed brow permanently etched on the base.
- **Happy**: Permanent rosy cheeks.
- **Cool**: Sunglasses always on.
- **Dark**: Shadowy aura.

## Trail Effects ($1.99 each)
- **Fire**: Leave a trail of smoke and sparks.
- **Sparkle**: Magical glitter trail.
- **Smoke**: Dense grey trail.

## Hit Effects ($1.99 each)
- **Confetti**: Colorful paper explosion on impact.
- **Plasma**: Blue electric surge.
- **Big Boom**: Larger, darker explosion.

## Victory Effects ($2.99 pack)
- **Crown**: Head wears a crown during the completion screen.
- **Fireworks**: Small fireworks launch from the head on 3-star victory.
